## Premier commentaire du maker

> *"A document that loses its shape loses half of what it was trying to say."*
>
> Hi Product Hunt, I'm one of the makers behind Précis, built by Trigenys Group.
>
> We started Précis because translating a document rarely fails on the words. It fails on everything around them: the two-column report that collapses into one, the slide where the title spills off the frame, the table that no longer lines up. Our team kept rebuilding layouts by hand after every translation, and honestly, that is not work anyone should be doing in 2026.
>
> So Précis keeps the document as it was. You drop a PDF, DOCX, PPTX, XLSX or TXT file (up to 100 MB), choose one of 23 languages (36 regional variants), and you get back a file in the same format, with fonts, columns, tables and images where they were. Scanned PDFs printed in English or French go through OCR first, up to 10 pages per upload, and labels inside figures are translated in the image itself. You can follow the translation page by page and check it before downloading anything.
>
> We also care about who can actually pay for it. Prices follow purchasing power in three zones: CFA francs in sub-Saharan Africa, where Mobile Money (MTN MoMo, Orange Money) works alongside cards; US dollars in emerging markets; and euros elsewhere. Everyone gets 5 free pages a month, with no subscription and no card on file.
>
> There is also a Chrome and Edge extension that translates the text and the images of a web page in place.
>
> We'd love to hear what breaks. Send us your hardest document, the one with the strange layout, and tell us how it came out.

## Réponses préparées

**How is it priced?**
> Free up to 5 pages a month, no subscription or card. Then Starter (100 pages a month, 2 GB of storage) or Pro (500 pages, 20 GB), or single pages as you go. A PDF page or a slide counts as one page; for Word, Excel and text it is one page per started block of 500 words. Prices depend on your zone: for example, Starter is €7.90 a month in Western Europe and North America, $4.90 in emerging markets and 1,500 FCFA in sub-Saharan Africa. Yearly billing is cheaper per month. Full table: /tarifs

**Why does the price change by country?**
> Prices follow purchasing power in three zones. The zone comes from your country, and the exact price in your currency is shown in the app before any payment. Payment fees are added at checkout and shown before you confirm.

**What happens to my documents?**
> Files travel over HTTPS. The service is hosted by OVHcloud in Canada. The text is sent to our translation provider, DeepSeek, to be translated, and providers may process data outside your country. You keep all rights to the document and its translation. Temporary translation files are kept 7 days at most so a translation can resume; documents saved in your space stay until you delete them, and the free plan does not store documents at all. Every access by our team to a document is logged. If a document is under a confidentiality obligation, check that a translation provider is acceptable before uploading. Details: /confidentialite-documents

**How is it different from DeepL or Google Translate?**
> For a small free document, Google Translate is fine (10 MB, PDFs up to 300 pages), but it shows scanned pages without translating them. DeepL supports the most formats and lets you edit the result, but the free plan is one file a month up to 5 MB. Précis is built for heavy files (100 MB), scanned PDFs in English or French, layouts that must stay intact, and payment in FCFA by Mobile Money. Translation quality depends on the language and the document, so we suggest trying your own file with each free plan. Sourced comparison: /comparatif

**Can I edit the translated PDF?**
> Précis returns a PDF, like the original. If you need to edit the text freely, translate the Word version of the document if you have it.

**What if I run out of pages mid-document?**
> You can still run the full translation. Unpaid pages stay visible in the preview with a watermark, the download contains the paid pages, and you can buy the rest from the preview.
